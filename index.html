Phone Trial

A throwaway test for the Build Manager build. It tries a few iPhone features and writes down what happened.

First add this to your Home Screen.

In Safari tap the Share button, then Add to Home Screen. Then open Phone Trial from its icon. Notifications only work from the icon (iOS 16.4 or later).

Storage test
